# Near-pass sound effects

CC0-1.0 source effects:

- GO_s0unds123, “Bullet Fly By”: https://freesound.org/people/GO_s0unds123/sounds/730351/
- craigsmith, “S20-43 Heavy ammunition bullets flying overhead & hitting things”: https://freesound.org/people/craigsmith/sounds/675948/ — archival Hollywood optical/magnetic sound effects digitized from USC Cinema preservation tapes.

These are credited sound-design sources. They are not claimed to be independently verified live-fire field recordings. The old 79 ms qubodup 855248 excerpt is no longer used for enemy near-passes; its own description expresses uncertainty about its cause.

Processing: short excerpts, mono 48 kHz PCM, microphone/optical rumble removal, submillisecond onset fades and short release fades, restrained normalization. No synthesized oscillator, pitch shifting, playback-rate randomization or time stretching. Source URLs, exact excerpt boundaries, cue attack times and SHA-256 hashes are retained in `provenance.json`. Rebuild with `tools/build_bullet_flyby_audio.py` using the documented public HQ previews.
