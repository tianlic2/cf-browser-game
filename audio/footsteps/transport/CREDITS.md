These excerpts come from congusbongus’s [Footsteps on different surfaces](https://opengameart.org/content/footsteps-on-different-surfaces) for C-Dogs SDL. They were trimmed, level-matched and converted to mono WAV for this game.

- Steel derives from Eelke’s [fboots on aluminum ladder 01](https://freesound.org/people/Eelke/sounds/462598/).
- Wood derives from swuing’s [footstep-wood.wav](https://freesound.org/people/swuing/sounds/38876/).
- The wet layer derives from EminYILDIRIM’s [Water Footsteps](https://freesound.org/people/EminYILDIRIM/sounds/608663/) and swuing’s [footstep-concrete.wav](https://freesound.org/people/swuing/sounds/38873/).
